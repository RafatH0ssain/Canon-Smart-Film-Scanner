About the loupe

Click anywhere on the image to move the loupe. This enlarges pixels the camera already discarded when it downscaled the live-view stream, so it magnifies what you have rather than revealing more.

Camera zoom
Camera zoom versus the loupe

This one is real magnification: the camera crops the sensor and sends those pixels instead, so 5× and 10× show detail the loupe cannot. Only offered when the body supports it.

Peaking sensitivity 50%
About peaking

Sharp edges are marked red. Works in autofocus mode, unlike the camera's own peaking — so you can drive focus and see peaking at the same time.

About the sharpness readout

Press Measure in the toolbar (or M) and the reading appears over the picture. Relative only — turn focus and watch it peak. The four corner values are laid out where those corners are: read them together, and a film plane that is not parallel to the sensor shows up as one corner lagging the rest.

Why a capture takes a while

Most of it is the download: tens of megabytes over Wi-Fi, not the shutter. The status line reports which stage it is in and how long it has been going, so slow-but-healthy does not read as a hang.
